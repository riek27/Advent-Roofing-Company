Who We Are

Empowering communities.
Building sustainable futures.

EmpowerKids-South Sudan is a non-governmental organization committed to empowering children and communities through education, agriculture, youth development, and sustainable solutions. Founded with a vision of hope and resilience, we operate across Juba, Malakal, and Yambio, reaching vulnerable populations with transformative programs.

Our flagship Empower Farmers South Sudan (EFSS) program utilizes the Foundations for Farming approach to promote conservation agriculture, food security, and dignified livelihoods for smallholder farmers.

Empower Farmers South Sudan 🌱
