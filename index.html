Who We Are

Verified impact.
Radical transparency.

EmpowerKids–South Sudan is a non‑governmental organisation registered under the NGO Act 2016 (RRC Reg. No. 169). Founded in 2014, we operate across Juba, Malakal, and Yambio, delivering programmes in education, agriculture, youth empowerment, and civic voice. Our work is validated by CAF America, allowing US donors to give tax‑deductibly.

Every figure on this page is taken directly from our programme monitoring records. Funders and evaluators can request the underlying data at any time — we have nothing to hide.

Governance

Board & Leadership

EmpowerKids–South Sudan is guided by a strong governance framework and led by a dedicated management team committed to delivering impact. The Board of Directors provides strategic oversight and accountability, while the Executive Leadership drives implementation and day-to-day operations.

Executive Leadership

Featured Programme

Empower Farmers South Sudan 🌱

Using Foundations for Farming conservation agriculture on our 50×100 m demonstration farm in Gumbo‑Sherikat. Our 21‑person field team has trained 54 smallholder farmers, and we've harvested 472.44 kg of maize from just 0.132 ha — proof that climate‑smart farming works.

  • Demonstration farm & hands‑on training
  • 54 farmers trained across Bilnyang & Mangala
  • Conservation agriculture & food security
